# Worked examples

These examples demonstrate the visual-analogy method. They are patterns, not templates to copy.

## Enforcement strength as shape acceptance

### Meaning contract

- The system progresses from prose guidance to deterministic construction.
- Stronger enforcement reduces the agent's discretion at the controlled boundary.
- A shared contract aligns agents before execution.
- A named gate checks the result; a mechanical design prevents invalid construction.

### Mapping

| Source concept | Visual element | Intended inference |
|---|---|---|
| Prose | Unmarked opening and independently shaped pieces | Each participant interprets the request. |
| Schema contract | Shared outline or gauge | Participants agree on the accepted shape. |
| Named gate | Inspector or checkpoint | A discrete check explicitly accepts or rejects. |
| Mechanical | Jig or keyed opening | Invalid output cannot physically pass. |

### Revision lesson

Place consequences with the mechanism that causes them. A note about divergent conventions belongs
with prose, not with the schema contract. Use opposing axis labels only when the direction is
unambiguous at a glance.

## Guarantee and failure-mode duality as ship diagnostics

### Meaning contract

- One guarantee can fail through different mechanisms.
- Each mechanism needs a detector capable of observing that mechanism.
- A joint failure, state failure, and material failure remain distinct even when the outcome is the
  same.

### Mapping

| Source concept | Visual element | Detection mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| Seam leak | Straight manufactured joint with one failed section | Dye test or inspection trace |
| Open valve | Contained onboard tank and pipe with an open valve | Position indicator or valve state |
| Hull crack | Jagged break in an otherwise continuous plate | Ultrasonic or structural inspection |

### Revision lesson

Do not color an impossible liquid red merely because red means failure. Separate semantics: water
keeps its physical color, coral marks the faulty component, and teal marks the detector or signal.
When detection is the teaching point, omit dramatized flooding and show the diagnostic mechanism.

## Failure and mitigation as a pair

Use a pair when the combined image rushes the audience through the problem.

1. The first visual establishes the failed state and lets the audience form a solution hypothesis.
2. The second preserves the same scene and adds the mitigation.
3. The delta between images carries the explanation; unrelated scenery must not change.

Score the pair as one interface. Repeated context that helps comparison is not duplication; repeated
labels or decoration that do not clarify the delta are common-reading tax.
